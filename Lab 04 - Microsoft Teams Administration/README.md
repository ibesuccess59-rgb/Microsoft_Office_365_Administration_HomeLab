## Lab 4 - Microsoft Teams Administration

## Objective 
- Create and configure a Microsoft Team for departmental communication, collaboration, and file sharing.
- Manage Team membership and roles by adding users and assigning appropriate Member and Owner permissions.
- Create and organize Team channels for Help Desk support and IT project collaboration.
- Configure and verify Team permissions by restricting channel management for standard members and testing access using both Member and Owner accounts.

## Scenario
We are Microsoft 365 administrators for a small company. The IT department needs a Microsoft Team where employees can communicate, collaborate, and share files.
