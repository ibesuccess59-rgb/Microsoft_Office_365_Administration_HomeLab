## Lab 05 - Sharepoint Online & Permissions Administration

## Objectives
- Create and configure a SharePoint Team Site for secure HR departmental collaboration and document management.
- Manage SharePoint membership and access by assigning appropriate owners and members to the HR site.
- Create and organize a document library using departmental folders for structured HR document storage.
- Upload and verify documents and permissions by testing SharePoint access from an HR employee account.

## Scenario 
Our company needs a secure HR SharePoint site where HR employees can store and collaborate on documents. We need to create the site, upload files, organize documents, and control who has access.
